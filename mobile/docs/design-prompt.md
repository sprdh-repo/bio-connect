Built-in ImageGen tool, `ui-mockup` use case.

> Asset type: high fidelity visual design reference for a Flutter attendee app, Bio Connect 4.0, Kerala 2026.
> Primary request: Create one polished presentation image showing three adjacent modern phone screens: Home, Explore, and Speaker profile.
> Make them realistic app UI concepts with coherent components, clear visual hierarchy, generous touch targets, sophisticated editorial typography, accessible contrast, and practical mobile navigation.
> Home should feature Bio Connect 4.0, 08-09 October 2026, Hyatt Regency Trivandrum, a biotech-themed hero, Register button, explore themes and speaker preview.
> Explore should show five themes: Biopharma, MedTech, Nutraceuticals, Agri-Food Innovation, Artificial Intelligence.
> Speaker profile should show portrait, name, role, and information hierarchy.
> Match website visual language: deep forest green #0b3329, cream #f3f1e9, lime #b9dc72, gold #e4ad54; restrained science imagery and subtle grid motif.
> Avoid generic neon gradients, fake complex charts, illegible microcopy, extraneous features, logos you invent.
> This is a design reference, not final screens.
> White or cream neutral background outside phones.

The reference contains illustrative speaker information.
