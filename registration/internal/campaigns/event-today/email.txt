__GREETING__

Your pass and how to get in.

Bio Connect 4.0 is under way at Hyatt Regency Trivandrum, and we look forward to welcoming you.
Your admission pass is attached to this email.
Here is how to get in quickly and make the most of both days.

YOUR PASS
Show the QR code on the attached PDF on your phone or on a printout.
You can also keep it in the official app: open My passes, tap Add a pass and verify with __EMAIL__.
Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.kerala.bioconnect
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bio-connect-4-0/id6817779744

GETTING IN
1. Print your badge. Scan your pass QR at a self-service kiosk or the registration desk. Printing your badge checks you in.
2. Wear your badge. Keep it on throughout the venue, the sessions and the expo.
3. Bring it back on Friday. Scan the same badge to check in for day two without printing a new one.
