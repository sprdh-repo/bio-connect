__GREETING__

Your pass, programme and people. All in one place.

Thank you for registering for Bio Connect 4.0.
Install the official app before you arrive to carry your admission pass on your phone, plan your two days and keep track of everyone you meet.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.kerala.bioconnect
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bio-connect-4-0/id6817779744

ADD YOUR PASS TO THE APP
1. Install Bio Connect 4.0 from Google Play or the App Store.
2. Open My passes, tap Add a pass and verify with this email address, __EMAIL__, or the mobile number you registered with.
3. Show the admission QR on your phone at the registration desk.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
Your pass, on your phone: keep your admission QR saved in the app, ready at the entrance.
My agenda: bookmark sessions, see what is on now and next, and get reminders.
Scan to connect: scan a badge to save a contact, add notes and export them later.
Speakers and expo: browse speakers, the programme and every exhibitor on the floor.
Selfie frame: frame a selfie with "I'm attending" and share it as a post or story.
Moments: find your photos from the official event album with a single selfie.

GOOD TO KNOW
You choose what you share: in My passes, decide whether your email and phone are shared when someone scans your badge.
Nothing is shared unless you opt in.
Prefer not to use the app? The PDF pass we emailed you remains valid at the entrance.
