Kerala Rubber Limited works to strengthen Kerala’s natural rubber industry through value-added manufacturing, with an initial focus on micro, small and medium enterprises.

Its vision brings together growers, entrepreneurs and public-private investment to develop industrial infrastructure, create opportunities and support a sustainable future for the sector.
