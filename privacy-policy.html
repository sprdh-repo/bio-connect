BIO CONNECT 4.0

Privacy policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how the Bio Connect 4.0 event organisers use information through the Bio Connect 4.0 Android and iOS apps and the associated event registration service at reg.bioconnect.kerala.gov.in. For privacy questions, contact bioconnect@bio360.in.

Information we collect

You can browse the event guide, speakers, sessions, activities, venue details and FAQs without creating an attendee account. When you register, we collect the attendee's name, email address, phone number, designation, institution or organisation, registration category and optional WhatsApp consent. If you register someone else, you must have permission to provide their information.

The associated registration website may also collect exhibitor contact information, company descriptions and logos, attendee rosters, payment references, payment dates, amounts and payment evidence that you submit. We keep registration status, pass identifiers, delivery status and organiser review records to operate the event.

Our servers and infrastructure providers process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata to deliver content, secure the service and limit abuse. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

How we use information

  • Register attendees and exhibitors, review registrations and verify reported payments.
  • Issue and deliver passes, manage pass validity and support event admission.
  • Send registration confirmations, recovery links and essential event communications.
  • Deliver passes and event updates through WhatsApp when you opt in.
  • Respond to support requests, correct information and protect the service against misuse.

We do not sell attendee personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Who receives information

Authorised organisers and event staff can access information needed for registration, payment review, support and admission. Service providers process information needed to provide hosting, storage, email and messaging services: Amazon Web Services for infrastructure, Postmark for email delivery, and Meta/WhatsApp for WhatsApp messages when enabled with your consent. Information may be processed outside your country through these providers.

When you open a payment service, map, brochure or other external website, that service's privacy policy applies. Payment services process payment information directly; the app does not collect card details or banking passwords. We may disclose information when required by law or to investigate fraud and protect the service.

Exhibitor names, approved company descriptions and logos may be published in the event directory. Attendee contact details and private payment evidence are not published in that directory.

Storage and security

Requests to our production service use HTTPS. Access to registration records is restricted to authorised personnel, and private uploads are stored separately from public event content. The service uses authentication and access controls for staff and private registration links for attendees. Keep your registration links and passes private. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Registration and related records are retained for event administration, support, payment reconciliation, accounting, dispute resolution and applicable recordkeeping obligations. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for registration records. Deletion is handled by the organisers on request, subject to records that must be retained for these purposes. Retained records remain subject to access controls; backup copies may remain until their backup retention cycle ends.

To request access, correction or deletion of your information, or withdraw optional WhatsApp consent, email bioconnect@bio360.in with the subject “Bio Connect privacy request”. Include your registration reference, if available, and send the request from your registered email address where possible. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity before acting and will explain if any records need to be retained. You do not need an app account to make a request.

Device storage and permissions

The app includes public event content for offline use and may hold loaded content and form information while it is running. Removing the app clears its local app data, but does not delete registration records held by the organisers. Directions open in an external map service; the app does not collect your precise location.

Children

This is a professional event app and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or our data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Privacy contact for Bio Connect 4.0: bioconnect@bio360.in.
