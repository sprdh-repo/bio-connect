About the conclave

A global platform for ideas that move life forward.

Bio Connect 4.0 brings together global industry leaders, researchers, startups and policymakers to redefine the future of biotechnology, pharmaceuticals and medical research.

Organised by Kerala Life Sciences Industries Park under KSIDC, the two-day conclave and expo creates meaningful connections between science, enterprise and public purpose.
